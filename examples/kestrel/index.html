How a flight works. Two pilots, one van, no rope team.

  1. 01Park
  2. 02Fly
  3. 03Find
  4. 04File

01 · Park

Lock the rotor with one blade level. The pilots launch from the van; nobody climbs.

02 · Fly

Four passes, root to tip. LiDAR holds K-1 six meters off the surface in gusts up to 12 m/s.

03 · Find

Defects are boxed while it flies. Cracks, erosion, and lightning damage, down to 0.2 mm, checked by an engineer before release.

04 · File

The report is filed by evening. Ranked by what to fix first, ready for your repair contractor.

The report lands the same day. Every finding located to the meter, sized to the tenth of a millimeter, and ranked by what to fix first.
