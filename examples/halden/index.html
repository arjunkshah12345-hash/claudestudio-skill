Halden

The place

The road ends at the farm. The fjord carries on for another forty kilometres.

Halden sits on the one flat strip of land between Bøen farm and the water. The mountain walls rise fourteen hundred metres on both sides, so the light arrives late and leaves early, and in summer it barely leaves at all. Fourteen rooms, one long table, a sauna you reach by jetty.

02 Sauna

A sauna on the water. The fjord is the cold plunge.

A wood-fired room for ten at the end of the jetty, with a glass wall at bench height so you watch the water while you sit. A ladder goes straight down into it.

03 Kitchen

Twenty seats. The farm is three hundred metres up the hill.

Bøen sends down what is ready in the morning, and the menu is written after it arrives. One sitting at 19:00. Guests eat first; four seats are held each night for the valley.

An evening in October

  1. Cured mackerel, sour cream, dill flowers
  2. Jerusalem artichoke baked in the embers, brown butter
  3. Lamb from Bøen, the last kale, juniper
  4. Apples from the orchard, brown cheese, sweet cicely

Five courses · NOK 1 150 · Non-residents Thursday to Saturday
